FAQ

General

Is there a subscription? No. Every tool is a one-time purchase — no account, no server, no recurring charge. Yours to keep and run offline for as long as you keep the file.

Which apps does it work in? Excel, Google Sheets, and Apple Numbers for the workbooks — tested, not assumed. The Paperwork Filler runs in any browser.

Is this legal, financial, or production advice? No. Slate & Ledger is not a law firm and these aren't legal, financial, or production advice — they're research and planning estimates. Always confirm a figure directly with the union, guild, agency, or a qualified entertainment attorney before relying on it for a real production. Full terms: Terms of Sale.

Do the tools talk to each other? Yes, where it's real data: the Schedule Builder's export feeds real shoot dates into the Budget Builder's Actuals tab and the Paperwork Tracker's weekly deadlines; the Residuals Tracker is a companion to the budget builders' own cast/crew data.

Pricing

Each tool is $35 (Residuals & Self-Distribution Revenue Tracker is $15), or get all five in the suite bundle for $99 — bought separately that's $155, so the bundle pays for itself once you need two or three of them.

Pay-what-you-want is available on the flagship tool and the bundle, with a real floor ($49 for the bundle). Students pay a reduced price on any tool or the suite ($15 / $39) — a .edu address or a photo of a student ID is enough. Genuine hardship: email us and tell us what you're making — no form, no means-testing, a human reply.

What happens when union rates update? A new dated Edition ships as a separate, low-cost upgrade ($15 single tool / $39 suite) for owners of the immediately prior Edition. Your current Edition keeps working regardless.

Are you affiliated with any festivals? We're building relationships with a small number of festivals to offer their filmmakers a discount on the suite. If your festival is interested in partnering, email hello@slateandledger.net.

Micro-Budget Film Budget Builder

Do I still need Movie Magic Budgeting? No — built to replace it at this budget size. Non-union? Every tier has a Non-Union path. More on the product page.

Episodic Series Budget Builder

Is this just the film tool with an episode counter? No — per-episode/season cost modeling and the per-episode tier-cap check are genuinely episodic concerns a single-project tool can't represent. More on the product page.

Schedule Builder

Does it read a PDF script? No, on purpose — .fdx and Fountain parse reliably; a PDF breakdown is always a rough guess. Will Auto-arrange give a perfect schedule? No — it's a reviewable first pass, not a final answer. More on the product page.

Paperwork Tracker (+ the Filler)

Does this file anything for me? No — it fills and generates the paperwork; you (or your Business Representative, or the union's own portal) still submit it. More on the product page or the Help page.

Residuals & Self-Distribution Revenue Tracker

Do I need this if I already bought a budget builder? Only if you want to model what the film earns after release — it's a genuinely separate calculation. More on the product page.
