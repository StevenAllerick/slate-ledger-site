Quick start

How it all connects

Each kind of note has its own place because the app uses it later. Your thoughts become a track you can listen to, your images become a guided visualisation, and the words you circle become what you're tested on. The prep you do on the page is what you learn from.

  1. Bring in your script

    Tap New project. Upload the PDF, or tap Scan pages to photograph paper sides, then choose who you play. If the app misread a line, hold it and fix it.

  2. Prep: your creative work, sorted so it can be used later

    The icons along the top switch each kind of note on and off. Tap the margin beside any line to add one.

    • ? Questions: what you need to figure out. Your answers feed everything below.
    • Given circumstances (the page icon): who, where, when, and what just happened. Later, Listen can read them to you before the scene.
    • Thoughts: what's going through your head on each line. They become your Thought line, and can show on screen while you run.
    • ✦ Imagine: the pictures in your mind. They become your Imagine playback.
    • ⚑ Moments: the beats that have to land, and how you'll work on each one. Track them from Open to Landed.
    • Notes: anything else. They can come up before your lines in a run.
    • Pens: circle or underline your key words. Fade out and Key words use them.

    Drag any of these to another line. Switch them all off for a clean page.

  3. Learn: built from your own notes

    • Imagine plays back the images you wrote, woven through the scene. Best with your eyes closed.
    • Thought line plays the chain you built: their line, your thought, then your line.
    • Fade out hides words from the page. You choose whether your circled and underlined words stay as anchors or go first.
    • Key words shows only the words you marked, the backbone of each speech.
    • Build it up: learn one line at a time, then join it to the lines before. Try "Hear, then say." Say "Line" if you need a prompt.

  4. Run the scene

    Pick a style and tap Start run. Voices read the other parts, and the app listens and comes in on cue. It works with your phone locked. You can choose to see your thoughts and notes as you go. Lines you miss become Trouble spots. Tap See them in Prep to go back to the notes under them.

    • Listen plays the scene while you walk or commute, with your thoughts and given circumstances too if you want them.
    • In Setup, you can choose each character's voice, or record a friend reading their lines.

  5. Tape with a reader who isn't in the room

    Use this when you're filming on another camera or phone. On Run, tap Teleprompter, then Another device. Put this phone beside the camera as your prompter. Your reader joins On a call with a code or link, from any phone or laptop with nothing to install. They read the other parts, and your words move along as they scroll. You can also let Thoughtline's voices read instead.

  6. Self-tape on this phone

    On Run, tap Self-tape. Your lines sit just under the lens, and the reader plays softer than you so casting hears you. Add a slate, record, then pick your best take, trim it, and share it or save it to Photos.

Tip: tap i at the top of any screen in the app for help on that screen. Questions or ideas? Email hello@slateandledger.net, or, if you're a beta tester, take a screenshot in the app and tap "Share Beta Feedback".
