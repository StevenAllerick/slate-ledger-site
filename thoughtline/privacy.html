Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026 · The Allerick Dynasty Inc.

Thoughtline is an app that helps actors learn their lines. It is made by The Allerick Dynasty Inc., a California corporation (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information Thoughtline handles, where it goes, and what you can do about it.

The short version: your scripts, notes and recordings stay on your device. We don’t run ads, we don’t track you across other apps or websites, and we don’t sell or share your information.

Your script text only leaves your device when you choose a feature that needs it. Section 3 lists those features.

1. What stays on your device

These are stored only on your iPhone or iPad, inside Thoughtline’s own storage:

  • Scripts and sides you bring in, and the text Thoughtline reads out of them.
  • Your work on them: thoughts, notes, marks, given circumstances, moments, cuts and practice progress.
  • Recordings you make, including audio of your lines and self-tape videos.
  • Settings, such as pace, voices and display choices.

We can’t see any of this. If you delete Thoughtline, it’s deleted from your device too. A backup file you saved somewhere else is the only exception.

2. The microphone and speech recognition

Thoughtline asks for microphone access for two things: recording your lines, and, if you choose, hearing when you’ve finished a line so it can move on to the next one.

  • Recordings are saved on your device only.
  • Listening for your lines uses Apple’s speech recognition. On devices that support it, this runs entirely on your device. On older devices that can’t do this, Apple may process the audio on its own servers, under Apple’s privacy policy. Your voice is never sent to us.
  • The microphone is only on while you are recording, or rehearsing with listening turned on.

You can turn microphone access off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone. Recording and listening won’t work while it’s off, but everything else will.

3. Features that send information off your device

These only run when you choose them.

Studio voices (18 and over)

Thoughtline’s free voices are Apple’s built-in voices, and they run on your device. Studio voices are optional, paid AI voices, offered only to people 18 and over. Thoughtline asks your permission before it first sends anything to make them. You can withdraw that permission at any time in Setup, under Privacy & account.

When you use studio voices, the text of the lines being read aloud is sent through our server to Google’s Gemini text-to-speech service, which turns it into audio. That’s usually the other characters’ lines, plus yours if you choose. The audio comes back to your device and is saved there, so each line only needs to be made once.

Our server doesn’t store the script text, and our logs never record it. Google processes the text under its paid API terms. Those terms say Google doesn’t use it to train its models, but may keep it for a limited time to detect abuse.

Sign in with Apple and your voice balance

When you buy studio voices, we keep a record of what you bought and how much of it you’ve used, so your balance works on all your devices. The record is tied to an anonymous ID from Sign in with Apple, and we use that ID for nothing else.

We don’t ask Sign in with Apple for your name or email address. Your scripts and recordings are never part of this record.

Purchases

Apple handles all payments. We never see your card or payment details. Apple tells us which purchases you’ve made so we can unlock them.

Rehearsing with a reader (13 and over)

  • A reader with you, or a reader on a call: while the session is open, the scene’s lines and your place in the script pass through our server to your reader’s phone or browser. They’re deleted when the session ends. Anyone with the link or code can see the scene while it’s open, so only share it with your reader.
  • FaceTime (SharePlay): audio and video go through FaceTime, which is Apple’s service. Thoughtline shares the scene’s lines and your place in the script with the other people on the call.

Backups and sharing

When you export a backup or share something, it goes wherever you send it, such as Files, AirDrop or Mail. After that it’s in your hands.

4. What we don’t do

  • No advertising, and no ad or tracking tools.
  • We don’t sell or share your personal information, as California law defines “selling” and “sharing.”
  • We don’t track you across other companies’ apps and websites, and we don’t let any other company track you through Thoughtline. Because we don’t track you, Thoughtline works the same whether or not your device sends a “Do Not Track” signal.
  • Your scripts, notes and recordings are never used to train AI models, by us or by anyone we work with.
  • We don’t read your scripts, notes or recordings. Apart from the features in section 3, they never reach us.

5. Companies that help us

  • Apple: the App Store and payments, Sign in with Apple, speech recognition, Apple voices, and FaceTime/SharePlay.
  • Google: Gemini text-to-speech, for studio voices only.
  • Cloudflare: runs our small server for studio voices, voice balances and reader sessions.

Each of them handles information under its own privacy policy, and only for the job we use it for.

6. How long we keep information

  • Information on your device: until you delete it or delete the app.
  • Script text sent for studio voices: not stored by our server. It’s passed to Google and dropped once the audio is made.
  • Reader sessions: deleted when the session ends.
  • Voice balances: as long as you have a balance, or until you delete your account. After that we keep only what tax and accounting law requires.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to answer you, and no longer than two years.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can delete projects, notes and recordings in the app at any time.
  • You can turn off microphone access and studio voices whenever you like.
  • Deleting your account: in Setup, under Privacy & account, choose Delete My Account. This deletes your voice-balance record from our server and disconnects Sign in with Apple. Any unused voice balance is lost.
  • You can ask us what information we hold about you. For most people, that’s only a voice-balance record. You can also ask for a copy, a correction or deletion. Email hello@slateandledger.net and we’ll respond within 30 days. We won’t treat you differently for asking.
  • If you’re in the EU or UK:
    • We use your information to provide the features you choose (performing our contract with you), to meet tax and accounting law (legal obligation), and to answer your messages (our legitimate interests).
    • Sending lines to Google for studio voices is based on your permission.
    • We’re based in the United States, so your information is processed there.
    • You can object to how we use your information, and you can complain to your local data-protection authority.

8. Children

Thoughtline is a general app for actors, including young actors, and it limits features by age:

  • Under 13: only features that run on the device. No studio voices, no readers through our server, no Sign in with Apple, and nothing is sent to us.
  • 13 to 17: everything except studio voices.
  • 18 and over: everything.

Thoughtline sets this up using the age range Apple shares with apps, or a simple birth-year question. Your age stays on your device. Until it’s answered, Thoughtline treats you as under 13.

If you think a child under 13 has sent us personal information, email us and we’ll delete it.

9. Security

Information that leaves your device is sent encrypted (HTTPS). Our server keeps as little as possible, for as short a time as possible. No system is perfectly secure, but keeping your work on your device is the strongest protection we can offer.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. For important changes, we’ll also tell you in the app before they take effect.
